# Certify Native with a frozen repeatable benchmark

Native Quality is certified in Chrome at fixed 60 Hz after a 60-second prewarm, using a frozen Reference Experience manifest and a deterministic ten-minute route that covers open sea, hero-vessel interaction, storm, waterline/underwater, and temporal-stability stress. Five consecutive runs must all pass; cold-start and first-effect activation are tested separately, detailed GPU telemetry is disabled by default and A/B checked for observer overhead, and any change to hardware class, OS, browser, Three revision, manifest, assets, or pass sizes expires the prior result. Missing the gate blocks Native release: TAAU, dynamic resolution, hidden effects, or redefined metrics may not convert a failure into a pass.

The frozen pass conditions are average displayed FPS and tail-mean 1% low at least 30; p99 displayed interval at most 34 ms; p99.9 at most 50 ms; p99 GPU and CPU frame-production time each at most 28 ms; hitch ratio below 5 ms/s; dropped or partially presented active frames at most 0.1%; no consecutive dropped frames; no post-prewarm interval above 100 ms; at most one cadence miss in the worst rolling second; and at most 3% relative spread in tail-mean 1% low across the five runs.
