# Separate Artistic Controls from prewarmed structure

Artistic Controls such as wave character, foam, spray, color, absorption, and scattering may change smoothly while the runtime is ready. Structural choices such as effect availability, cascade count, resource formats and sizes, render-pass layout, anti-aliasing route, and material or MRT variants belong to the immutable Prewarm Manifest; changing one returns through the Loading Experience and creates a fresh lease instead of compiling at first use or performing incremental startup mutation.
